## Who this notice covers

This notice explains how the company named on this page ("we" or "us") handles your personal data when you use this website and when you contact us about travel. It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

In short:

- this website does not store what you type into its forms or send it anywhere
- we receive your details only when you send them to us yourself, or call us
- we use them only to answer you and arrange the travel you ask for
- we do not sell your details or add you to a mailing list
- you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, and complain if you are not satisfied

## What this website collects

This website does not ask you to create an account.

The site has three kinds of form: an enquiry form on service, holiday and contact pages, a call-back request, and the "Plan my trip" guided request. They ask for your name and phone number, and, depending on the form, your email address, travel dates, number of travellers, budget, destination ideas, company name or GSTIN, and a message. Each form says beside its button what the details are for.

When you press the button, your browser checks the answers and prepares a message on your own device. The website does not send that message anywhere and keeps no copy of it. The next screen offers two ways to send it to us yourself: "Send on WhatsApp", which opens WhatsApp with the message filled in, and "Email instead", which opens your email app with it. Nothing reaches us unless you send it.

Like most websites, the hosting service keeps short technical logs of requests, such as the address of the device and the page asked for, to run the service and keep it secure.

## Cookies and browser storage

The website sets no advertising, analytics or other non-essential cookies, and it loads no tracking scripts. Fonts and photos are served from the website itself.

While you fill in "Plan my trip", your answers are kept in your browser's session storage, so they are not lost if you refresh the page. They stay on your device, are cleared as soon as you have prepared the request, and are removed by your browser when you close the tab.

## When you contact us

You choose how to reach us: by WhatsApp, by email or by phone. When you contact us, we receive what you choose to share, such as the details listed above. WhatsApp and your email provider handle the message under their own privacy terms.

## Documents for bookings, visas, insurance and foreign exchange

Some services need more than an enquiry:

- bookings need each traveller's name as it appears on their passport or ID, and sometimes their date of birth, nationality and contact details
- visa, passport and FRRO applications can need passport copies, photographs, and financial papers such as bank statements and income tax returns
- travel insurance can need the age and health details of each traveller
- foreign exchange and trips abroad need your passport, ticket and PAN, as the law requires

We ask for these only when you choose one of these services, and use them only for that booking or application. We ask for your clear consent before we collect financial or health details.

## Why we use your details

We use your details:

- to reply to your enquiry or call you back, as you asked
- to plan and quote for your trip, and make the bookings and applications you ask for
- to send you travel documents, updates and changes about a booking you have made
- to keep accounts, issue invoices and collect taxes such as GST and TCS (tax collected at source), as the law requires
- to answer a request from a court or a government authority, where the law requires it

We rely on your consent, or on the details you chose to give us for a stated purpose. Where the law requires us to keep or share a record, we do so because of that law. We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for marketing lists.

## Who receives your details

To serve you, your details may reach:

- WhatsApp and email providers, when you send us a message through them
- the company that hosts this website, which keeps the short technical logs described above
- airlines, hotels, cruise lines, rail, car and coach operators, and local tour operators, for the booking you ask for
- insurers and foreign exchange providers, for the policy or currency you ask for
- embassies, visa centres and the authorities that decide visa, passport and FRRO applications
- our accountants, auditors and tax authorities, for our accounts and tax filings

Each of them receives only what it needs for that purpose.

## Details shared outside India

Some of the people above are outside India, for example a hotel, a tour operator or an embassy abroad, or the servers of a messaging or hosting service. We share your details abroad only for the trip, application or service you ask for, and never to a country that the Government of India has restricted.

## How we keep your details safe

We take reasonable security measures to protect your details. Only the staff who handle your enquiry or booking can see them, and we keep documents for an application only as long as that application needs them, unless the law asks us to keep a record.

If a breach of your personal data happens, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

## Children

Our forms are meant for people aged 18 or over. A parent or lawful guardian gives us any details we need about a child who is travelling, such as a passport copy for a family visa, and consents on the child's behalf. We use a child's details only for that trip or application.

## How long we keep your details

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to answer you, and booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and deal with any claim or refund. Accounts and tax records, such as invoices, are kept for the period the law sets, usually up to eight years. When we no longer need your details, we delete them.

## Your rights

You can:

- ask us what details we hold about you, how we use them, and who we have shared them with
- ask us to correct, complete or update them
- ask us to delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them
- withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it
- name another person to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself

Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it. If you withdraw consent for a booking that is in progress, we may not be able to complete it.

You also have duties under the law: please give us correct details, and do not pretend to be someone else.

## How to ask, and how to complain

To use any of your rights, or to ask a question or make a complaint about how we handle your details, write to our Grievance Officer using the details on this page, or use any of the ways on our [contact page](/contact). We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request.

We will reply as soon as we can, and within one month at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

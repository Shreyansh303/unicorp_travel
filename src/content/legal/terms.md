## About these terms

These terms apply when you use this website and when the company named on this page ("we" or "us") arranges travel for you. Please read them before you confirm a booking. For a booking, your written quote and confirmation also apply, and where they differ from these terms, the quote and confirmation apply.

## About this website

This website gives information about our holidays, corporate travel and travel-related services, and ways to contact us. You cannot book or pay for anything on it.

## Our role

We are a travel agency. We plan your trip and arrange services that other companies provide: airlines, hotels, cruise lines, rail, car and coach operators, local tour operators, insurers and foreign exchange providers. Each of them provides its service under its own terms, including its rules on changes, cancellations and refunds, and those terms apply to your booking. We tell you about the terms that matter before you confirm.

## Packages and prices

Holiday packages are examples of trips we can plan. Prices are estimated "starting from" prices per person, on the basis stated with each price. The actual price depends on your dates, the number of travellers, availability and supplier rates at the time of booking.

Prices exclude GST, and TCS (tax collected at source) on trips abroad. Both are charged as the law requires and shown in your quote. To collect TCS, the law requires us to take your PAN.

A price or itinerary on this website is not an offer. We confirm the final itinerary, price and payment terms to you in writing before anything is booked.

## Quotes and bookings

A quote is valid for the time stated in it. Until a booking is confirmed, prices and availability can change, because airlines and hotels change their fares and rooms all the time. Prices for services abroad can also change with exchange rates until they are paid for.

A booking is made only after you accept a written quote and pay what it asks for. It is confirmed when we send you a written confirmation, after the supplier has confirmed it.

## Payments

Your quote sets out what to pay and when, including any advance and the date the balance is due. If a payment is not made on time, the supplier may cancel the booking, and its cancellation charges may apply.

Pay only to the bank account of the company named on this page, or through a payment link we send you in writing. We will never ask you to pay into a personal account. If you receive payment details you are unsure of, call us on a number on this website before you pay.

## Service charges

Our service charges, where they apply, are shown in your quote. They are for the work of planning and arranging your travel, and the quote says whether they are refundable if you cancel.

## Changes and cancellations by you

Please ask for any change or cancellation in writing, by email or WhatsApp. Changes depend on what the supplier allows, and may cost more.

If you cancel, the supplier's cancellation charges apply, along with any of our service charges that the quote says are not refundable. Some fares and rates, such as many airfares and special hotel offers, cannot be refunded at all. We tell you the charges before you cancel.

## Refunds

When a supplier refunds money for your booking, we pass it on to you after we receive it, less the supplier's charges and any of our charges that your quote says are not refundable. We refund to the account you paid from. Refunds can take as long as the supplier takes to pay them, and we will keep you informed.

## Changes and cancellations by suppliers

Airlines, hotels and other suppliers sometimes change schedules, rooms or services, or cancel them. When that happens, we tell you as soon as we know, and help you with the options the supplier offers, such as another date, another service or a refund.

## Your documents and responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- giving each traveller's name exactly as it appears on their passport or ID, and checking every ticket and voucher we send
- holding a passport that is valid for the trip, usually for at least six months after your return, with enough blank pages
- getting the visas, permits, vaccinations and health documents each country on your route needs
- reaching the airport, station or meeting point on time
- following the laws of the places you visit, and the rules of the airlines and hotels you use

We tell you what we know about these requirements, but rules change, and the decision to let you travel or enter a country rests with the airline and the authorities.

## Visas, passports and permits

We help you prepare visa, passport, FRRO and other applications. We are an independent agency, not part of any embassy, visa centre or the Passport Seva service. The embassy or the authority concerned makes every decision, and we cannot promise an approval or a processing time. Government and visa centre fees are paid to them, and they do not refund them if an application is refused.

## Travel insurance

The insurer issues every policy and decides every claim, under the policy wording. Please read the policy, including its limits and exclusions, before you buy, and tell the insurer about any health condition it asks about. We help you with the claim process, but the decision is the insurer's.
